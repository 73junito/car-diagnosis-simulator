Contact AutoLearnPro

AutoLearnPro and TorqueMind are currently operating as an educational automotive-diagnostics demonstration platform.

Technical feedback

Non-sensitive technical problems and feature requests may be submitted through the public TorqueMind issue tracker .

Public issues must not contain passwords, authentication codes, security vulnerabilities, financial information, medical information, private student information, educational records, or other sensitive personal information.

Privacy and student-data requests

Do not submit privacy requests or identifiable student information through the public issue tracker. A dedicated private contact channel will be published before production student accounts or identifiable student records are enabled.

Security concerns

Do not publicly disclose active credentials or sensitive vulnerability details. The project is configuring a private security-reporting channel.
