Safety & limitations

Training model, not a physical sensor-service procedure

This lab presents a governed training model. Sensor states, injected faults, student-entered measurements, calculated transfer values, and signal traces describe the modeled system only. Vehicle-specific service information and approved shop procedures remain authoritative for physical diagnosis and repair.

        Guided practice

        1. Identify the 12 V vehicle supply and the 5 V training reference.
        2. Trace reference supply from the regulator to the sensor.
        3. Trace the analog signal from sensor to controller.
        4. Trace the sensor ground/reference path.
        5. Compare open, high-resistance, and short-circuit faults.
