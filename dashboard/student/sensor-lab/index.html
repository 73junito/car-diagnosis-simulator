Guided practice

  1. Identify the 12 V vehicle supply and the 5 V training reference.
  2. Trace reference supply from the regulator to the sensor.
  3. Trace the analog signal from sensor to controller.
  4. Trace the sensor ground/reference path.
  5. Compare open, high-resistance, and short-circuit faults.

Evidence boundary

The 5 V reference shown here is a common training example, not a universal sensor specification. Actual sensor type, supply voltage, signal range, pinout, ground strategy, diagnostic limits, and procedures require authoritative vehicle-specific service information.
