Guided practice

  1. Identify the battery as the electrical source.
  2. Locate the main charging protection.
  3. Trace the B+ connection between battery and alternator.
  4. Trace the alternator ground return.
  5. Inject an open charging-feed fault and trace the path again.

Evidence boundary

No vehicle-specific pin numbers, voltage limits, procedures, or manufacturer wiring diagrams are represented here. Vehicle-specific authoritative service information is required before those details may be shown.
