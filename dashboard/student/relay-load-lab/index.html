Guided practice

  1. Identify the battery and the two protected branches.
  2. Trace the control switch to the relay coil.
  3. Distinguish the relay coil from the relay contact.
  4. Trace the load-current path through the relay contact.
  5. Inject a fault and identify whether it affects control or load current.

Evidence boundary

This project-authored template teaches general relay-circuit relationships only. Actual relay strategy, terminal designations, cable routing, protection, current limits, voltage-drop limits, and procedures require authoritative vehicle-specific service information.
