Interactive 12 V Relay-Controlled Load Lab

Compare the relay control side with the separately protected load side, trace conventional current, and inject faults without assuming vehicle-specific wiring or limits.

Learning goal Explain how a low-current control branch energizes a relay coil so the relay contact can switch a separate load-current branch.

Safety & limitations

Training model, not a physical relay-service procedure

This lab presents a governed training model. Relay states, injected faults, student-entered measurements, calculated values, and current-flow traces describe the modeled system only. Vehicle-specific service information and approved shop procedures remain authoritative for physical diagnosis and repair.

      Diagnostic reasoning

      Inspect, observe, explain

      Select a symbol or test point to inspect its role.

      Guided practice

      1. Identify the battery and the two protected branches.
      2. Trace the control switch to the relay coil.
      3. Distinguish the relay coil from the relay contact.
      4. Trace the load-current path through the relay contact.
      5. Inject a fault and identify whether it affects control or load current.

      Model status · explicit limitations

      Evidence boundary

      This project-authored template teaches general relay-circuit relationships only. Actual relay strategy, terminal designations, cable routing, protection, current limits, voltage-drop limits, and procedures require authoritative vehicle-specific service information.
