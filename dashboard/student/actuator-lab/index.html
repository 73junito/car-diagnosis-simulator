Safety & limitations

Training model, not a physical actuator-service procedure

This lab presents a governed training model. PWM duty examples, calculated averages, injected faults, student-entered measurements, and circuit availability describe the modeled system only. Vehicle-specific service information and approved shop procedures remain authoritative for physical diagnosis and repair.

      Diagnostic reasoning

      Inspect, observe, explain

      Select a symbol or test point to inspect its role.

      Guided practice

      1. Identify the actuator power, PWM command, and ground paths.
      2. Trace the 12 V actuator power feed separately from the controller command.
      3. Compare the ECM PWM source with the actuator-side PWM test point.
      4. Inject an open or short fault and identify the affected path.
      5. Explain why duty-cycle or PWM-average data alone does not prove actuator response.

      Model status · explicit limitations

      Evidence boundary

      This project-authored template teaches generic PWM actuator relationships only. Actual actuator architecture, duty cycle, frequency, current, position, speed, force, flow, pinout, wiring, diagnostic limits, and procedures require authoritative vehicle-specific service information.
