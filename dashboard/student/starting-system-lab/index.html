Guided practice

  1. Identify the 12 V nominal battery source.
  2. Trace the protected start-command path.
  3. Identify the solenoid coil and main switching contact.
  4. Trace the high-current path to the starter motor.
  5. Inject a fault and compare the affected path.

Evidence boundary

This project-authored template teaches general circuit relationships only. Exact starter architecture, cable routing, protection, terminal designations, voltage-drop limits, procedures, and specifications must come from authoritative vehicle-specific service information.
