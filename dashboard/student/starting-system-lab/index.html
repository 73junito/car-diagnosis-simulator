Guided practice

  1. Identify the 12 V nominal battery source.
  2. Trace the protected start-command path.
  3. Identify the solenoid coil and main switching contact.
  4. Trace the high-current path to the starter motor.
  5. Inject a fault and compare the affected path.

Evidence boundary

This project-authored template teaches general circuit relationships. The Interstate battery ratings and Delco Remy starter/cable limits shown above are source-specific reference records with their own applicability. Selecting them together does not establish product compatibility and does not replace authoritative vehicle-specific service information for actual wiring, procedures, limits, or component selection.
