Interactive 12 V Starting-System Lab

Trace the protected start-command path and the separate high-current starter-motor path using the standardized electrical symbol, connection, and voltage-domain libraries.

Learning goal Explain how a start command energizes a starter-solenoid actuator and permits battery current to reach the starter motor.

Safety & limitations

Training model, not a physical starting procedure

This lab presents a governed training model. Operating states, injected faults, source-backed reference values, calculations, and animation describe the modeled system only. Vehicle-specific service information and approved shop procedures remain authoritative for physical diagnosis and repair.

      1. Identify the 12 V nominal battery source.
      2. Trace the protected start-command path.
      3. Identify the solenoid coil and main switching contact.
