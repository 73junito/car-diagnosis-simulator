# TorqueMind diagnostics skill

- Use established automotive and OBD-II terminology.
- Do not invent DTCs, PID definitions, measurements, or specifications.
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses, tests, results, and conclusions.
- Require evidence before increasing diagnostic confidence.
- Prefer the next discriminating test over premature component replacement.
- Do not reveal the final diagnosis when guided discovery is required.
- Keep scoring and competency decisions deterministic and rubric-backed.
