# Elevation

Elevation communicates hierarchy without overwhelming the interface.

## Surface Levels

- Level 0: page background
- Level 1: cards and panels
- Level 2: active panels, dialogs, dropdowns
- Level 3: overlays and modals

## Rules

- Use shadows sparingly.
- Prefer borders and spacing for basic separation.
- Interactive elements may use subtle hover elevation.
- Do not stack heavy shadows.
