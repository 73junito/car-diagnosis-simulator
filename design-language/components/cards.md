# Cards

## Purpose

Cards organize related information.

## Variants

- KPI Card
- Scenario Card
- Transcript Card
- AI Coach Card
- Timeline Card
- Chart Card
- Alert Card

## Rules

- One purpose per card.
- Avoid nested cards.
- Use consistent padding.

## Card Anatomy

Header

Body

Actions

Footer

## Accessibility

Cards containing actions must provide keyboard access.

## Testing

- Responsive layout
- Action visibility
- Screen reader labels
