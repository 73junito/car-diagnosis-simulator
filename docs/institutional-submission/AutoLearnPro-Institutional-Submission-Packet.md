## 1. Purpose of this submission

AutoLearnPro is an evidence-aware automotive technology learning and diagnostic-practice platform. This packet organizes the current production, curriculum, privacy/AI, accessibility, source-governance, and assessment-governance evidence for an external institutional reviewer.

## 5. Assessment boundary

The current production release does **not** authorize:

- grading,

## 6. Source rights and evidence governance

AutoLearnPro maintains source-specific governance rather than treating all publicly accessible material as reusable.

The current source-rights baseline verifies:

- **38 governed sources**
- **0 unresolved source-rights classifications**
- source-specific citation/linking, paraphrase/summary, reproduction, database-storage, AI/RAG, and commercial-use controls
- fail-closed behavior for restricted sources
- external research-discovery metadata does not automatically become approved instructional or assessment evidence

The Phase 7C quality baseline additionally verifies 64/64 lessons have direct-domain authority under the repository's deterministic screening rules.

## 7. Privacy and AI controls

Current governance includes the following product invariants:

- student information is used only for documented educational/product purposes;
- student data is not sold or rented;
- student-derived data is not used for targeted advertising;
- institution-provided education-record data is not repurposed for unrelated commercial use;
- biometric identification is disabled by default;
- AI/model training on identifiable institution-provided student records is disabled by default;
- AI remains advisory for instruction and diagnostic reasoning;
- institution-specific retention, deletion, contract, subprocessor, age, and jurisdiction requirements must be resolved before deployment where applicable.

The compliance baseline addresses federal/Kansas requirements and implemented California, Illinois, and Texas overlays. It is an engineering/compliance artifact and not legal advice.
