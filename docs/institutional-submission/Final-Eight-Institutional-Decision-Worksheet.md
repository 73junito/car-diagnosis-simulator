# Institutional Decision Worksheet — Final Eight Courses

**Purpose:** Record institution-authorized decisions needed before any additional AutoLearnPro course-delivery batch can be defined.

## Review boundary

This worksheet is for **human/institutional decisions only**. AutoLearnPro must not infer standing, placement, program approval, advisor approval, prerequisite substitution, equivalency, or advanced-course selection from learner data or page availability.

A blank, incomplete, or unsigned decision remains **pending** and does not unlock delivery. Completing this worksheet does not authorize assessment, accreditation, academic credit, enrollment, or Kansas Board of Regents approval.
