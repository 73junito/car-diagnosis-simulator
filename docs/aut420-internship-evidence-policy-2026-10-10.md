# AUT 420 Internship Employment and Placement Evidence Policy

## Prerequisite

**Verified industry employment or approved internship placement required.**

AUT 420 requires evidence that the student either:

1. is currently employed in an appropriate industry setting; or
2. has an approved internship placement with a confirmed employer and start date.

Evidence submission does not automatically approve the internship. Employment/placement verification and academic internship approval are separate human or institutional decisions.

## Preferred primary evidence

Preferred evidence includes:

- official employment verification letter;
- signed internship agreement or contract;
- formal employer offer letter.

Primary evidence should identify the student, employer, position or role, employment or internship status, start date, and an identifiable employer or supervisor source.

## Acceptable alternatives

When primary evidence is unavailable, the reviewer may consider:

- official employer onboarding documentation;
- employer or HR confirmation email from an identifiable company source;
- internship schedule or assignment letter identifying the role, supervisor, employer/work location, and expected start date.

## Supporting evidence only

The following may support a submission but should not normally be the only evidence:

- employee ID or company account;
- pay stub;
- payroll or direct-deposit documentation.

## Normally insufficient by itself

The following should not normally satisfy the prerequisite alone:

- a bank deposit without identifiable employer documentation;
- a screenshot without identifiable employer information;
- an informal text message;
- a self-written statement.

## Privacy and redaction

Students should not be required to disclose unnecessary payroll or financial details. When a pay stub or payroll record is used, the student may redact information that is not necessary to verify employment, including:

- compensation amount;
- bank account information;
- Social Security number;
- tax-withholding information;
- unrelated personal information.

## Review outcome

A designated human/institutional reviewer records one of:

- `verified`;
- `needs-additional-evidence`;
- `rejected`.

The reviewer record should include the reviewer, review date, and reason or notes. The system should retain the minimum document details necessary for verification.

## Governance boundary

AutoLearnPro must not automatically approve AUT 420 eligibility from an uploaded document. Evidence verification does not change academic status, authorize assessment, or itself establish internship approval.
