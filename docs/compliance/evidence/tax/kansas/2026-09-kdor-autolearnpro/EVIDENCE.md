## Inquiry

The correspondence responds to an inquiry concerning the Kansas
sales-tax treatment of AutoLearnPro.

AutoLearnPro was described to the Kansas Department of Revenue as an
automotive online instruction product that includes an interactive
diagnostic simulator.

## Factual assumptions stated or relied upon by KDOR

KDOR's response relies on the understanding that:

1. AutoLearnPro is treated as prewritten computer software for purposes
   of the analysis.

2. AutoLearnPro is delivered or accessed online through a web browser.

3. The software is hosted on a remote cloud server.

4. The software program is not physically installed on equipment
   located in Kansas.

5. The interactive automotive diagnostic simulation functionality is
   part of the cloud-based software offering.

These factual assumptions are material to the agency's stated
conclusion and must remain associated with this evidence whenever the
classification is relied upon.

## Kansas-only scope

The correspondence addresses Kansas sales-tax treatment.

KDOR expressly stated that it could not address the taxability of the
product when sold to customers located outside Kansas.

Sales or subscriptions involving customers in other states therefore
require separate jurisdiction-specific analysis.

This evidence must not be interpreted as determining sales-tax
obligations outside Kansas.

## KDOR conclusion

Based on the information provided and KDOR's stated understanding of
the AutoLearnPro delivery architecture, KDOR advised that software
accessed through the internet and not physically loaded onto computers
or servers located in Kansas is not subject to Kansas sales tax.

KDOR further advised that the interactive simulation functionality
should not change the taxability determination when the software
remains cloud-based.

## Statutory reference identified by KDOR

K.S.A. 79-3603(s)

KDOR cited this provision in discussing Kansas taxation of prewritten
computer software and services involving modification, alteration,
updating, or maintenance of prewritten computer software.

## Compliance interpretation

This evidence must NOT be summarized internally or publicly as an
unconditional statement that:

"AutoLearnPro is tax exempt."

The evidence-supported description is:

"Based on the facts provided to the Kansas Department of Revenue,
AutoLearnPro's cloud-hosted, browser-accessed software, including its
interactive diagnostic simulator, was advised by KDOR as not subject
to Kansas sales tax when the software is not physically installed on
computers or servers located in Kansas."

The conclusion is fact-dependent and Kansas-specific.

## Architecture-change review trigger

Kansas sales-tax treatment should be reviewed again before relying on
this evidence if AutoLearnPro later:

- provides downloadable or locally installable software;
- installs software on customer-controlled equipment;
- installs software on computers or servers located in Kansas;
- materially changes its cloud/browser delivery architecture;
- materially changes how customers receive or possess the software;
- introduces a transaction type materially different from the facts
  presented to KDOR; or
- receives subsequent KDOR guidance affecting this classification.

## Interstate-sales limitation

This evidence does not determine the taxability of AutoLearnPro in
other states.

Each jurisdiction in which AutoLearnPro establishes relevant sales-tax
obligations or makes taxable transactions must be evaluated under that
jurisdiction's applicable law and administrative guidance.
