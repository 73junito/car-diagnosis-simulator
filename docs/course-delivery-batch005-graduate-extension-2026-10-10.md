# Phase 7F-I Batch 005 Graduate Extension Execution

## Purpose

Phase 7F-I continues graduate course delivery after the verified Phase 7F-H foundation batch while preserving the distinction between **course-page availability** and **institutional eligibility or equivalency decisions**.

AUT 520, AUT 530, and AUT 550 now have dedicated graduate instructional course pages.

## Batch 005 — graduate extension — built and verified

| Course | Title | Prerequisite | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AUT 520 | Advanced Vehicle Data Analytics | AUT 515 or equivalent | Human/institutional verification |
| AUT 530 | Advanced Electric Vehicle Systems | AUT 501 and undergraduate EV coursework or equivalent | Human/institutional verification |
| AUT 550 | Advanced Automotive Networks | AUT 501 or equivalent | Human/institutional verification |

All three use identity-preserving `canonical-catalog-course` mappings and governed graduate lesson plans.

AUT 530 requires special care: page availability does **not** establish that a learner has acceptable undergraduate EV coursework or that prior coursework is equivalent. That determination remains outside repository delivery state.

## Deferred courses and blockers

AUT 525 remains outside Batch 005 because no identity-preserving canonical catalog-development mapping is currently recorded.

AUT 420 remains deferred because verified industry employment or approved internship placement is a human/institutional eligibility condition.

AUT 120 and AUT 150 remain intentionally blocked from canonical delivery until dedicated canonical curriculum content is approved.

## Governance boundary

Phase 7F-I delivery execution adds three graduate instructional course pages and changes zero academic-status or assessment-eligibility fields. It does not authorize admissions, prerequisite or equivalency decisions, scoring, grading, high-stakes use, accreditation, Kansas Board of Regents approval, academic-credit authority, or institutional adoption.
