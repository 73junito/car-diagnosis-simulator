Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 12, 2026

AutoLearnPro provides educational automotive-diagnostics demonstrations, training scenarios, curriculum tools, and related learning services.

Information processed

Depending on the features used, the service may process account information, learning activity, scenario progress, assessment results, feedback, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used to provide learning features, maintain progress, improve the platform, troubleshoot problems, prevent misuse, and protect the service. Information is not sold for third-party advertising.

Browser storage

Demonstration features may store preferences or progress in the user's browser. Clearing browser storage may remove locally saved progress.

Student information

Schools or instructors using the platform with student accounts should establish an authorized agreement and appropriate privacy procedures before entering identifiable student information. The public demonstration should not be used to submit sensitive personal, educational, financial, or medical information.

Retention and deletion

AutoLearnPro applies documented retention limits rather than retaining student-related information indefinitely. Student-linked telemetry uses a 30-day logical retention boundary where that telemetry contract applies. For ordinary direct-user account closure, the operational target is to complete covered active-data deletion within 30 calendar days, subject to validated legal, security, contractual, or backup-handling exceptions.

Institution-controlled education records require an institution-approved retention configuration before institutional onboarding at scale. Deletion requests must also account for applicable exports, derived records, service-provider copies, and backups so completed requests are not silently reversed by a later restore.

Service providers and subprocessors

AutoLearnPro may use service providers for hosting, authentication, security, communications, analytics, or other platform operations. Institutional use is subject to the platform's reviewed subprocessor and data-governance controls. Providers are not authorized by this notice to use student information for unrelated advertising.

Access, correction, and deletion requests

Requests to access, correct, or delete information may be submitted through the AutoLearnPro contact page. Requests may require identity or authority verification and are handled according to applicable law, institutional agreements, retention requirements, and validated security or legal holds.

Data protection

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the AutoLearnPro contact page.
