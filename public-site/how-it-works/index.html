How it works

A visible path from concern to verification.

The learning model separates the stages of diagnostic work so students can practice the process deliberately and instructors can review how conclusions were reached.

  1. 01

    Define the concern

    Start with what the vehicle, system, or customer is presenting.

  2. 02

    Understand the system

    Review the relevant system, operating principles, and expected behavior.

  3. 03

    Gather evidence

    Use observations, measurements, references, and scenario information to narrow possibilities.

  4. 04

    Make the decision

    Select and explain the diagnostic conclusion supported by the available evidence.

  5. 05

    Verify the result

    Confirm the outcome and document what was checked, what changed, and what remains uncertain.

Important: Reference availability does not automatically make material eligible for assessment use. Source rights, instructional permissions, and assessment eligibility remain independently governed.
