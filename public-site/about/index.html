About AutoLearnPro

Automotive learning centered on diagnostic reasoning.

AutoLearnPro is an automotive learning platform designed to make the reasoning behind diagnosis visible, teachable, and reviewable.

Purpose

Connect curriculum, guided diagnostic practice, technical evidence, and verification so learners can explain not only what they chose, but why.

Instructional approach

Move from concern to system understanding, evidence gathering, decision-making, verification, and documentation in a structured sequence.

Governance

Keep source rights, instructional use, AI/RAG permissions, and assessment eligibility as separate decisions rather than treating one approval as approval for everything.

Important: AutoLearnPro is still being developed and reviewed. Availability of individual courses, scenarios, assessment features, and institution-level capabilities may vary as those components complete review and release gates.
