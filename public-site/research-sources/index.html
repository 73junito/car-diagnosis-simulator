Research Sources & Attribution

AutoLearnPro uses scholarly discovery services to help instructors and curriculum developers locate research that may be relevant to automotive technology and technical education.

Semantic Scholar

Research discovery metadata is powered in part by Semantic Scholar, a project of the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2).

Semantic Scholar results are treated as discovery metadata only. A returned paper, abstract, citation count, author record, or identifier does not automatically become approved curriculum evidence and does not create scored-assessment eligibility. Sources must pass AutoLearnPro's provenance and human-review requirements before instructional approval.

AutoLearnPro does not use the Semantic Scholar API to republish full copyrighted articles.

How research becomes governed evidence

Discovery is only the first step. AutoLearnPro keeps source discovery, provenance review, rights review, instructional approval, AI/RAG permission, and assessment eligibility as separate decisions.

  1. Discover: identify a potentially relevant source or authoritative record.
  2. Verify: confirm provenance, publisher or owner, stable identifiers, and technical applicability.
  3. Review rights: record whether linking, quotation, reproduction, commercial use, or AI/RAG ingestion is permitted.
  4. Approve instruction: require human review before a source is treated as approved instructional evidence.
  5. Gate assessment: instructional approval does not automatically make content eligible for scored assessment.

Source categories

The governed evidence workflow may include scholarly discovery metadata, government-hosted technical information, openly licensed educational resources, and standards or manufacturer material when its provenance, applicability, and rights have been reviewed. Availability in search results or in a reference list is not itself permission to copy, ingest, redistribute, or score against that source.

Reference-library boundary

The authenticated platform maintains a governed Reference Library that displays source-specific rights and audience information. Public research discovery and the authenticated Reference Library serve different purposes: discovery helps locate candidates; the governed library exposes only records that have passed the platform's applicable review gates.
