For students

Practice the process, not just the answer.

The student experience is built around structured automotive learning and diagnostic reasoning. Available tools may include learning paths, scenarios, guided feedback, technical references, and activity history as each capability is enabled.

Follow structured pathways

Work through course and lesson sequences that connect automotive concepts to diagnostic application.

Reason from evidence

Practice using symptoms, observations, measurements, and technical references before committing to a diagnosis.

Explain and verify

Build the habit of explaining diagnostic decisions and confirming the result instead of stopping at a selected answer.

Important: Assessment availability is controlled separately from learning content. A lesson, reference, or practice activity being visible does not by itself mean it is approved for graded assessment.
